Privacy

LANZA · Last updated October 21, 2026

LANZA is a throwing practice app for Ultimate Frisbee players. This page explains what the app stores, where, and why. The short version: your practice history lives on your phone, we only use your Strava account to post your sessions and back up your history, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your sessions (makes, throws, distance, target drawing, throw type, conditions, LANZA Score, session name), your settings (language, weekly goal, voice options) and your Strava sign-in are saved in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Strava

Connecting Strava is optional. When you connect, Strava gives LANZA permission to read your basic profile (name and photo) and to upload activities. LANZA uses it only to:

  • post your practice sessions to your Strava account and update them when you rename a session or change its conditions;
  • show your name and photo inside the app;
  • confirm it is really you when you share a session with your team or use the backup.

LANZA does not read your other Strava activities, routes, followers or any other athlete's data. You can disconnect at any time from the You screen in the app, which also revokes LANZA's access on Strava. You can also revoke it in Strava under Settings › My Apps.

Backup

If you connect Strava, a copy of your LANZA sessions and settings is stored on our server, linked to your Strava athlete number, so you can get your history back on a new phone. Only someone signed in to your Strava account can read it. You can delete it at any time with "Delete my backup" on the You screen, and you can download everything with "Download my data".

Team board

If you join a team, each finished session (its numbers, your display name and, if connected, that you are verified with Strava) is shared with the people who have that team code. Anyone with the code can see the board, so share it only with your team. Leaving the team stops sharing new sessions.

Microphone

Hands-free mode uses your browser's speech recognition to hear "make" or "miss". Audio is handled by your browser and its speech service; LANZA never records or stores audio.

Ads

LANZA may show an optional rewarded ad to unlock extra sessions. When ads are on, they are served by Google Ad Manager, which may use cookies or device identifiers as described in Google's ad policies. Ads are off unless the app owner turns them on.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use your Strava data for advertising or share it with anyone except your team, as described above.
  • We don't track your location.

Hosting

The app and its server functions are hosted on Netlify. Team boards and backups are stored in Netlify Blobs.

Deleting your data

Delete your backup from the You screen, leave your team, disconnect Strava and clear the app from your browser. To remove sessions already shared on a team board, contact us.
